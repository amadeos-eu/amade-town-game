# How the computer builds a town, on the title screen and in the tests: one step every few seconds, as a
# player would with the mouse. A step the town cannot pay for waits until the money is there.
#
#   mission  the mission whose map it builds on
#   every    seconds between two steps
#   do       road, line or bulldoze col row col row: along the columns first, then the rows
#            zone r|c|i col row col row: the rectangle between two corners
#            build building col row: with its top left corner on that tile
#            wait: a step of doing nothing
