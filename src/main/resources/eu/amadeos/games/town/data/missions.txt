# What each mission gives the town and what it asks of it.
#
#   map        the map in the folder maps, a text file of one letter per tile (see the README)
#   money      the money at the start, in dollars
#   goal       how many people the town must have to win
#   happiness  how happy they must be at the same time, in percent (any if not given)
#   years      the goal must be reached before this many years are over (no limit if not given)
#   fire       yes if houses catch fire
#   forbid     buildings the town may not build in this mission

mission 1

  money 20000
  goal 1000

mission 2
  map map2.txt

  happiness 60
  years 15
  fire yes

mission 3
  map map3.txt
  money 15000
  goal 3000
  happiness 65
  years 18
