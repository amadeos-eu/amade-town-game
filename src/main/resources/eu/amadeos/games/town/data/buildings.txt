# What the town can build besides roads, power lines and zones. Money in dollars, power in MW.
#
#   tool     the button of the panel: power, water, services or park
#   work     what it does: plant (coal), wind, pump, tower (water), school, library, clinic, fire, hall, park
#   size     width and height in tiles
#   cost     what it costs to build
#   upkeep   what it costs a month
#   power    the power a plant makes; for every other building, the power it uses
#   water    the water a pump gives
#   reach    how far it serves, in tiles; for a building that makes smoke, how far the smoke carries
#   smoke    the pollution it makes on its own tiles
#   shore    yes if it must stand next to water
#   one      yes if a town has one at most
